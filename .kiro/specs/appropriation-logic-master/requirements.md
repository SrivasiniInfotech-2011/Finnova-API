# Requirements Document

## Introduction

FINNOVA-67, **Appropriation Logic Master – Sequential Due Priority & Vertical/Horizontal Allocation Setup**, gives a Risk & Policy Administrator an auditable way to configure the rules that FINNOVA-62 Receipt Appropriation uses in Suggest mode and Suggest-mode Confirm. A rule belongs to one Company and either applies globally or to one Line of Business (LOB). Each rule contains an ordered sequence of due heads and percentages, plus Vertical or Horizontal distribution and Full Due behavior.

The feature covers the backend API in `Finnova-API` and the React + TypeScript + Material UI screen in `Finnova-UI`. FINNOVA-67 owns rule configuration, validation, activation, versioning, and audit. FINNOVA-67 does not calculate a receipt allocation, mutate due balances, post journals, or create canonical accounting entries. FINNOVA-62 remains the allocation and Confirm owner; FINNOVA-63/JVE remains the journal-posting boundary.

Finnova is India-only and English-only. The screenshot is authoritative for the legacy screen structure and label `Receipt Type`, but the persisted canonical concept is `AppropriationDueType` / `CashFlowDueType`. Values and examples use Indian business context, INR, and English due heads. The UI introduces no Arabic or other non-English values, bilingual columns, translation scaffolding, RTL handling, or Oman-specific content.

## Glossary

- **Appropriation_Logic_Master**: The FINNOVA-67 configuration service and persisted rule set for Company and LOB appropriation policy.
- **Appropriation_Rule**: One versioned configuration record containing Company scope, LOB scope, direction, Full Due, active state, and ordered details.
- **Company**: The authoritative tenant and legal-company context identified by `CompanyId`; every rule belongs to one Company.
- **Line_of_Business (LOB)**: An active business line identified by `LineOfBusinessId` and owned by the Company. The UI may show `All` for global scope.
- **Global_Rule**: A rule with a null LOB scope, displayed as blank or `All` according to the approved UI terminology, and applicable to all LOBs in the Company.
- **LOB_Rule**: A rule with one exact `LineOfBusinessId` scope.
- **AppropriationDueType**: The canonical internal due-head identity used by the rule and adapters. The initial values are `Installment`, `Principal`, `Interest`, `Insurance`, `ODI`, `BankCharges`, and `ChequeReturnCharges`.
- **CashFlowDueType**: The due-ledger representation of an appropriation due head. A mapping contract connects `AppropriationDueType` to the authoritative cash-flow due ledger; the rule does not use receipt payment types.
- **Receipt Type**: The exact legacy UI label for a configurable due head. `Receipt Type` means an appropriation/due head on this screen and does not mean FINNOVA-61 payment modes Cash, Cheque, or PDC.
- **Distribution_Direction**: `Vertical` or `Horizontal`. Vertical processes a contract’s configured due sequence before the next contract; Horizontal processes one configured due head across all eligible contracts before the next configured due head.
- **Full_Due**: A Boolean policy that makes the allocation consumer clear each eligible due in sequence up to its outstanding balance before advancing within the configured traversal, subject to receipt amount and the detail target.
- **Rule_Detail**: An ordered child record containing one due head, a percentage from 0.00 through 100.00, and a sequence number.
- **Sequence_Number**: A one-based rule-detail order generated by the service. A later sequence cannot be processed before the preceding sequence is satisfied according to the rule consumer’s target semantics.
- **Applicable_Rule**: Exactly one active rule selected for a Company and receipt LOB using exact active LOB precedence followed by the active global fallback.
- **Rule_Version**: An immutable version identifier captured whenever a rule is changed or consumed.
- **Appropriation_Resolver**: The read contract owned by FINNOVA-67 that returns one applicable active rule, its version, scope, direction, Full Due, ordered details, and resolution evidence to FINNOVA-62.
- **FINNOVA-62 Receipt Appropriation**: The feature that Shows dues, Suggests allocation, and Confirms due and Advance Account allocation using the FINNOVA-67 resolver contract.
- **FINNOVA-63/JVE**: The journal-posting boundary that consumes approved accounting submissions; FINNOVA-67 never writes journal tables.
- **Risk_and_Policy_Administrator**: The authorized SystemAdmin business user who maintains appropriation rules.
- **SystemAdmin**: An authenticated user with the SystemAdmin role and the required Company administration scope.
- **Audit_Entry**: An immutable record of a rule create, update, activate, deactivate, or conflict-resolution event, including actor, Company, scope, version, and timestamp.
- **Idempotency_Key**: A client request identifier that makes a retry of the same mutation return the original result without creating a duplicate version.
- **EARS**: Easy Approach to Requirements Syntax.

## Dependencies and Assumptions

- **Dependency D1 — FINNOVA-61 receipt context:** Receipt Processing remains the authoritative source of `CompanyId`, receipt LOB, customer, receipt amount, payment mode, and receipt status. FINNOVA-67 does not copy or override receipt payment types.
- **Dependency D2 — FINNOVA-62 resolver consumer:** FINNOVA-62 must consume the `Appropriation_Resolver` contract for Suggest and Suggest-mode Confirm. FINNOVA-62 must fail closed when resolution returns no rule or multiple applicable rules, and must capture rule ID/version and ordered details.
- **Dependency D3 — Due ledger mapping:** The authoritative due ledger supplies due heads and outstanding balances. The adapter must map `AppropriationDueType` to `CashFlowDueType` without conflating the rule with Cash/Cheque/PDC receipt payment modes.
- **Dependency D4 — Company and LOB masters:** Existing active Company and LOB references are reused. An LOB must belong to the selected Company before an LOB rule can be saved.
- **Dependency D5 — Hosting and gateway:** The backend slice is hosted in the existing SystemAdmin service or the existing SystemAdmin master-data host identified by repository conventions. The implementation must use the existing SystemAdmin gateway route and confirm the final route alias before coding. No new host is introduced by this specification.
- **Dependency D6 — Authorization:** SystemAdmin authentication, role policies, Company scope, and existing exception middleware are reused. Risk & Policy Administrator is represented by the approved SystemAdmin permission unless the authorization owner supplies a narrower existing policy.
- **Dependency D7 — Journal boundary:** FINNOVA-67 does not call a Journal Writer, JVE posting endpoint, due-balance update, or canonical accounting repository. FINNOVA-62 and FINNOVA-63/JVE retain those boundaries.
- **Assumption A1 — Global scope storage:** Company scope is authoritative. A global rule stores `LineOfBusinessId = null`; the UI may display blank or `All`. If the existing schema requires an `All` sentinel, the adapter must normalize that sentinel to the same global scope and must not allow a second global rule.
- **Assumption A2 — Scope uniqueness:** At most one active rule exists per Company plus global scope or exact LOB scope. Historical inactive versions may remain for audit, but an active scope cannot be ambiguous.
- **Assumption A3 — Sequence generation:** Sequence numbers are generated by the server as one-based contiguous order for a rule. Inserted details receive the next sequence; updates preserve or explicitly recompact order within one transaction. Clients cannot choose a sequence that skips a prior sequence.
- **Assumption A4 — Percentage semantics:** Each detail percentage is inclusive from 0.00 through 100.00. Duplicate due heads are rejected. The total may be below 100.00 and represents a residual that FINNOVA-62 routes to Advance Account. For deterministic allocation, a total above 100.00 is rejected. Product must confirm whether totals above 100.00 should instead be accepted as capped targets; this specification uses the safe maximum-total rule.
- **Assumption A5 — Zero percentage:** A zero-percent detail may be stored for policy visibility but is not an allocation target. The final implementation must apply the same duplicate and sequence validation to zero-percent details.
- **Assumption A6 — Sequence satisfaction:** FINNOVA-67 persists sequence order and policy metadata. FINNOVA-62 enforces the runtime rule that sequence N is exhausted or has no eligible amount before sequence N+1 is processed. FINNOVA-67 does not calculate balances.
- **Assumption A7 — Full Due meaning:** Full Due causes FINNOVA-62 to clear each matching due head/contract up to the outstanding amount before moving to the next eligible item, subject to the detail target and receipt remainder. FINNOVA-67 stores the Boolean and exposes it to the resolver.
- **Assumption A8 — Rule lifecycle:** A newly created rule is inactive unless the create request explicitly uses the approved activation workflow. Activation and deactivation create a new immutable version and audit entry; old versions are never edited in place.
- **Assumption A9 — Concurrency:** Optimistic concurrency uses a row version and expected version in mutation requests. A stale update returns 409 and creates no new active version.
- **Assumption A10 — Idempotency:** Create, update, activate, and deactivate accept a client idempotency key. Repeating the same key and normalized payload returns the original result; reusing the key with a different payload returns a conflict.
- **Assumption A11 — Audit immutability:** Every successful mutation creates one append-only audit entry. Failed validation, stale mutation, and read-only resolution create no business audit entry.
- **Assumption A12 — UI terminology:** The screen keeps the exact title `APPROPRIATION LOGIC`, the exact grid label `Receipt Type`, and the fields and footer named in the Jira screenshot. Internal API names use due-head terminology to prevent payment-mode confusion.
- **Assumption A13 — India localization:** Rule examples use `LN - Loan`, `Installment`, `Principal`, `Interest`, `Insurance`, `ODI`, `Bank Charges`, `Cheque Return Charges`, and INR. No Oman, Muscat, OMR, Arabic, bilingual, or RTL content is permitted.
- **Assumption A14 — API errors:** APIs return the platform ProblemDetails shape with stable English error code, message, correlation ID, and field context. Configuration conflicts use 409 or 422 according to the existing middleware mapping.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Company and LOB Scope

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want every rule tied to an authoritative Company scope, so that one tenant cannot consume another tenant’s appropriation policy.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL require `CompanyId` on every Appropriation_Rule and SHALL validate that the Company is active and within the SystemAdmin’s administration scope.
2. WHEN a rule is saved with a null LOB scope, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL classify the rule as a Global_Rule for the Company.
3. WHEN a rule is saved with an LOB scope, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL validate that the LOB exists, is active, belongs to the Company, and is not an `All` sentinel.
4. IF a request supplies an LOB belonging to a different Company or an inactive LOB, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject the request with a field-specific validation error and SHALL persist no rule version.
5. WHEN an LOB-specific rule and a global rule are active for the same Company, THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL return the exact active LOB rule for a receipt LOB before the global rule.
6. IF no active exact LOB rule and no active global rule exist for a Company and receipt LOB, THEN THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL return a typed no-applicable-rule result that FINNOVA-62 treats as a fail-closed configuration error.
7. IF more than one active rule exists for one Company and one exact scope, THEN THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL return a typed ambiguity result and FINNOVA-62 SHALL reject Suggest and Suggest-mode Confirm without allocation or audit mutation.

### Requirement 2: Screenshot-Aligned Administration Screen

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want the rule screen to match the approved legacy workflow, so that policy maintenance is recognizable and consistent.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Appropriation Logic page opens, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL display the exact title `APPROPRIATION LOGIC`.
2. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL display a `Line of Business` dropdown, a `Type` dropdown containing only `Vertical` and `Horizontal`, a `Full Due` checkbox, and an `Active` checkbox.
3. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL display a section titled `APPROPRIATION LOGIC DETAILS` with a grid containing `S.No.`, `Receipt Type`, `Percentage`, `Edit`, and `Delete` columns.
4. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL provide a new-row due-head selector and Add action in the details area.
5. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL display footer actions named `Save`, `Clear`, and `Cancel`.
6. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL display the legacy label `Receipt Type` for due-head rows while the accessible field description and API payload identify the value as an AppropriationDueType.
7. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL display English India-specific examples and INR amounts and SHALL display no payment-mode values Cash, Cheque, or PDC in the due-head selector.
8. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master UI SHALL render left-to-right and SHALL not add RTL, Arabic, Oman, Muscat, OMR, bilingual, or translation behavior.

### Requirement 3: Rule Direction and Full Due

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want to choose the allocation direction and Full Due policy, so that FINNOVA-62 can apply contractual priority consistently.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a rule is saved with Type `Vertical`, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL persist `Distribution_Direction = Vertical`.
2. WHEN a rule is saved with Type `Horizontal`, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL persist `Distribution_Direction = Horizontal`.
3. IF a rule request contains a direction outside `Vertical` and `Horizontal`, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject the request with a validation error and SHALL persist no version.
4. WHEN Full Due is checked, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL persist `FullDue = true` and SHALL expose the value through the resolver contract.
5. WHEN Full Due is unchecked, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL persist `FullDue = false` and SHALL expose the value through the resolver contract.
6. WHEN FINNOVA-62 consumes a Vertical rule, FINNOVA-62 SHALL complete the configured due-head sequence for Contract 1 before processing Contract 2.
7. WHEN FINNOVA-62 consumes a Horizontal rule, FINNOVA-62 SHALL process Due Type 1 across all eligible customer Contracts before processing Due Type 2.

### Requirement 4: Ordered Rule Details

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want ordered due-head details, so that each due priority is explicit and reviewable.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL store every Rule_Detail with one AppropriationDueType, one percentage, and one positive Sequence_Number.
2. WHEN a Rule_Detail is added, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL assign the next available Sequence_Number for the rule and SHALL ignore any client-supplied sequence value.
3. WHEN a Rule_Detail is deleted, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL preserve the remaining detail order and SHALL return a contiguous one-based sequence to the client.
4. WHEN a rule is read through the resolver, THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL return details ordered by Sequence_Number ascending.
5. WHEN FINNOVA-62 applies a resolved rule, FINNOVA-62 SHALL not process Sequence N+1 while an eligible target from Sequence N remains under the selected direction and Full Due policy.
6. IF a rule contains no Rule_Detail after normalization, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject activation and SHALL return an English configuration error.

### Requirement 5: Percentage and Due-Head Validation

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want safe percentage and due-head validation, so that rule configuration cannot create ambiguous or over-targeted allocation behavior.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL accept each Rule_Detail percentage from `0.00` through `100.00` inclusive with no more than two fractional digits.
2. IF a Rule_Detail percentage is negative, greater than `100.00`, or has more than two fractional digits, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject the detail with a field-specific validation error.
3. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL allow the sum of Rule_Detail percentages to be less than `100.00` and SHALL expose the unallocated percentage to FINNOVA-62 as residual policy context.
4. IF the sum of Rule_Detail percentages is greater than `100.00`, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject activation and SHALL return `ERR-APPROP-RULE-TOTAL-OVER-100`.
5. IF a rule contains duplicate AppropriationDueType values, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject the request with `ERR-APPROP-DUPLICATE-DUE-HEAD` and SHALL persist no version.
6. WHEN a valid detail total is below `100.00`, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL not invent a missing detail or force a percentage increase.
7. WHEN FINNOVA-62 consumes a valid rule with a detail total below `100.00`, FINNOVA-62 SHALL leave the unallocated receipt amount as residual and SHALL route positive residual to Advance Account according to FINNOVA-62 rules.

### Requirement 6: Due-Head Mapping and Payment-Mode Separation

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want due-head values mapped to the authoritative due ledger, so that the legacy label cannot be confused with receipt payment modes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL expose an explicit mapping from AppropriationDueType to the authoritative CashFlowDueType or due-ledger code.
2. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL support the India-specific initial due-head values `Installment`, `Principal`, `Interest`, `Insurance`, `ODI`, `Bank Charges`, and `Cheque Return Charges`.
3. IF a due-head mapping is missing, inactive, or unsupported by the authoritative due ledger, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject activation and SHALL return a dependency configuration error.
4. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL not store FINNOVA-61 payment modes `Cash`, `Cheque`, or `PDC` as Rule_Detail values.
5. WHEN FINNOVA-62 receives rule evidence, THE FINNOVA-62 response SHALL include the canonical due-head identity, mapped ledger identity, sequence, and percentage for every applied detail.

### Requirement 7: Lifecycle, Active Rules, and Versioning

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want rule changes to be versioned and safely activated, so that receipt allocations can be traced to the policy in force at confirmation time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a valid rule is created, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL persist an immutable Rule_Version with its Company scope, LOB scope, direction, Full Due, ordered details, active state, actor, and UTC timestamp.
2. WHEN a rule is updated, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL create a new Rule_Version and SHALL leave the prior version immutable.
3. WHEN a rule is activated, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL validate all scope, mapping, sequence, duplicate, percentage, and direction constraints before marking the new version active.
4. WHEN a rule is deactivated, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL mark the active version inactive and SHALL create an immutable audit entry.
5. IF an active rule already exists for the same Company and scope, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL reject activation or replace the prior active version only through one atomic version transition that leaves at most one active version.
6. WHEN a rule has no active version, THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL exclude the rule from applicability resolution.
7. IF FINNOVA-62 confirms a Suggest allocation, THEN FINNOVA-62 SHALL capture the resolved Rule ID, Rule_Version, scope, direction, Full Due, and ordered details on the confirmed Receipt Appropriation and audit record.

### Requirement 8: Concurrency and Idempotent Mutations

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want concurrent edits and retries handled safely, so that rule history and active scope remain consistent.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a mutation includes the current expected Rule_Version or row-version token, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL apply the mutation only if the stored version still matches.
2. IF a mutation uses a stale expected version, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return HTTP `409` with `ERR-APPROP-RULE-STALE` and SHALL create no new version or audit entry.
3. WHEN a mutation includes a new Idempotency_Key and succeeds, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL associate the key with the resulting Rule_Version.
4. WHEN the same Idempotency_Key and normalized mutation payload are retried, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return the original result and SHALL create no second version or audit entry.
5. IF the same Idempotency_Key is reused with a different normalized mutation payload, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return HTTP `409` with `ERR-APPROP-IDEMPOTENCY-CONFLICT` and SHALL preserve the original version.
6. WHEN two valid activation requests race for one Company and scope, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL commit at most one active version and SHALL return a conflict for the losing request.

### Requirement 9: Immutable Audit and Read Contracts

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want every policy change and resolution to be traceable, so that financial-policy decisions can be reviewed later.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a rule create, update, activation, or deactivation succeeds, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL append exactly one immutable Audit_Entry containing actor, CompanyId, LOB scope, rule ID, Rule_Version, operation, correlation ID, and UTC timestamp.
2. IF a rule mutation fails validation, concurrency, dependency, or idempotency checks, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL append no successful-mutation Audit_Entry.
3. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL expose a read contract containing rule ID, Rule_Version, CompanyId, optional LOB, direction, Full Due, active state, ordered details, timestamps, and audit-safe actor information.
4. THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL return one resolution envelope containing resolution status, selected rule identity/version, matched scope, fallback indicator, direction, Full Due, ordered details, and a resolver contract version.
5. WHEN FINNOVA-62 stores a confirmed Suggest appropriation, THE FINNOVA-62 audit record SHALL retain the FINNOVA-67 resolution envelope or an immutable equivalent sufficient to reconstruct the applied policy.

### Requirement 10: Stable API Contracts and Errors

**User Story:** As a SystemAdmin client, I want stable APIs and errors, so that the UI and FINNOVA-62 can integrate without interpreting database details.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL expose list, get, create, update, activate, deactivate, and resolver operations with versioned JSON contracts.
2. THE resolver operation SHALL accept authoritative `CompanyId` and receipt `LineOfBusinessId` context and SHALL not accept a client-supplied rule ID as an authority override.
3. IF a request omits a required identifier, direction, detail, percentage, idempotency key, or expected version, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return HTTP `400` with stable field errors and SHALL persist no data.
4. IF an active rule is missing, ambiguous, or invalid for runtime resolution, THEN THE resolver SHALL return a typed result that FINNOVA-62 can fail closed without selecting a fallback outside the stated precedence.
5. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return only English error messages with stable codes including `ERR-APPROP-RULE-NOT-FOUND`, `ERR-APPROP-RULE-AMBIGUOUS`, `ERR-APPROP-RULE-STALE`, `ERR-APPROP-DUPLICATE-DUE-HEAD`, and `ERR-APPROP-RULE-TOTAL-OVER-100`.
6. WHEN the API returns a successful resolver response, THE response SHALL include `resolverContractVersion` so FINNOVA-62 can apply a compatibility gate.

### Requirement 11: SystemAdmin Authentication and Scope

**User Story:** As a security stakeholder, I want only authorized SystemAdmin users to change appropriation policy, so that policy cannot be altered by receipt operators.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. IF a rule-maintenance request has no valid JWT Bearer token, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return HTTP `401` and SHALL persist no rule or audit entry.
2. IF an authenticated caller lacks the approved SystemAdmin policy, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return HTTP `403` and SHALL persist no rule or audit entry.
3. IF an authenticated SystemAdmin lacks administration scope for a Company, THEN THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return HTTP `403` and SHALL persist no rule or audit entry.
4. WHEN the resolver is called by FINNOVA-62’s approved service identity, THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL authorize the service call without granting the caller rule-maintenance mutation permissions.
5. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL not trust CompanyId, LOB scope, actor, or active state from an untrusted display-only field when resolving authorization or audit identity.

### Requirement 12: Acceptance Scenarios

**User Story:** As a Risk & Policy Administrator, I want the Jira scenarios to persist and resolve predictably, so that policy setup can be verified before receipts are processed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a SystemAdmin creates an active LOB rule for `LN - Loan` containing ordered details Installment `40.00%`, Insurance `20.00%`, ODI `20.00%`, and Legal Charges `10.00%`, with Type `Vertical`, Full Due checked, and Company/LOB scope valid, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL persist sequence numbers 1 through 4, the exact percentages, `Vertical`, `FullDue = true`, active state, and one immutable audit entry.
2. WHEN the LOB rule from the preceding scenario is resolved for the same Company and LOB, THE Appropriation_Resolver SHALL return that rule before a global Company rule and SHALL return the four details in Installment, Insurance, ODI, Legal Charges order.
3. WHEN a SystemAdmin saves a valid rule and then retrieves the rule, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return the saved Company scope, LOB scope, direction, Full Due state, active state, ordered details, Rule_Version, and audit metadata.
4. WHEN a valid rule is saved twice using the same Idempotency_Key and identical normalized payload, THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL return one Rule_Version and one successful mutation Audit_Entry.
5. WHEN FINNOVA-62 performs Suggest for a receipt whose exact active LOB rule is the LOB rule above, THE FINNOVA-62 preview SHALL contain the resolved rule ID/version, `Vertical`, Full Due, ordered due-head details, and percentage total `90.00%`; any receipt residual after eligible dues shall remain available for Advance Account according to FINNOVA-62.

### Requirement 13: Testing and Operational Constraints

**User Story:** As a delivery team, I want automated coverage and safe ownership boundaries, so that the master can be delivered without changing accounting behavior accidentally.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master implementation SHALL include backend unit, property, integration, authorization, concurrency, idempotency, and audit tests and frontend Vitest/React Testing Library tests for the UI workflow.
2. WHEN property-based tests cover pure normalization, precedence, sequence, or resolver logic, THE test suite SHALL execute at least 100 generated cases per property using the repository-approved library.
3. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL not create or modify canonical journal entries, JV posting records, receipt payment modes, due balances, or receipt allocations.
4. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL use the shared Finnova UI HTTP client and existing MUI, React hooks, route, service-toggle, and authorization conventions.
5. THE Appropriation_Logic_Master SHALL preserve the exact screenshot labels required by Requirement 2 while keeping API and persistence names canonical and payment-mode-safe.
